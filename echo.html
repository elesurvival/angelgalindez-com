What Echo Is

Echo is a companion intake system for Continuo. It captures voice or manual entries from mobile and sends them into the larger continuity system where they can be organized, connected, and developed.

Why It Exists

I have too many valuable thoughts to rely on memory alone. Echo exists for the gap between having an insight and sitting down to organize it.

It is built for life in motion.
