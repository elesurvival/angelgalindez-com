The Story Begins

A mystery. A legacy. A call to adventure.

When Louie discovers his grandfather’s hidden laptop in the attic, he unlocks more than just a puzzle. He uncovers a legacy of knowledge, secrets, and a mission to protect the digital world.

With his friends by his side, Louie must solve codes, follow clues, and outsmart those who would use technology for the wrong reasons.

Behind the Story

Why I wrote The Code Keepers

I wanted to create the kind of adventure I would have loved as a kid, one that makes learning exciting, shows the power of curiosity, and inspires kids to think critically about the world around them.

This story is for every child who asks questions, loves puzzles, and dreams of making a difference.

— Angel Galindez
