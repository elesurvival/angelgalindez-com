Featured Reflection

Most people don’t need a Continuo. They have five ideas. I have five ideas before breakfast.

That sentence became more than a joke. It clarified why Continuo exists: not as another productivity app, but as a continuity system for people whose ideas arrive faster than life gives them time to process.
